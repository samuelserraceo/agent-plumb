Trademark-safe by design

All three concepts deliberately avoid Nintendo's Mario iconography: no red cap, no "M" logo, no blue overalls, no white gloves, no moustache, no Italian-plumber stereotype. Chartreuse + dark ink palette is uniquely ours. The plumb-bob (the actual carpenter's tool) is the central prop instead of pipes/wrenches — ties to the brand name + sidesteps any "plumber" cliché.
